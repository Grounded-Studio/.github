## 🧰 What we build

### 🏗️ [BuildExact](https://grounded-studio.app/buildexact.html)
Construction calculator and quote PDF generator for contractors.
**11 material calculators** — fence, tiles, concrete, insulation, roof, stairs, paving, timber, bricks, plasterboard, paint — plus client management and branded quote PDFs with digital signature.
Works offline. No ads. No tracking.
→ [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.metenweten.bouw)

### 🥬 [FreshGarant](https://grounded-studio.app/freshgarant.html)
Food expiry tracker with AI fridge scanner, AI meal planner, price-drop alerts and referral rewards.
Save money, cut food waste at home.
→ [Learn more](https://grounded-studio.app/freshgarant.html)

### 🎮 [Circuit Trace](https://grounded-studio.app/games/circuit-trace.html)
Minimalist one-finger puzzle game. 100 handcrafted levels, daily and weekly puzzles, 60-second time trial.
No ads during gameplay. Six languages. Currently in beta.
→ [Beta page](https://grounded-studio.app/games/circuit-trace.html)
